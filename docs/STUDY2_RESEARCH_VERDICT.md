# Study 2 research verdict

**NO-GO: stop before CR-EC-QAD training.** All 480 preregistered SIGNAL rollouts completed. Neither primary q interval excludes zero. No alternate formula or weighting search was performed; TEST remains unopened.

1. **Does raw covariance predict compression-critical conditions?** Not established beyond the specified covariates. Old covariance C and unit-dependent trace(P) have future/excess-future intervals crossing zero. This does not prove the absence of all nonlinear covariance information.
2. **Does teacher sensitivity predict them?** Sensitivity predicts instantaneous action discrepancy: 16.3769% prediction-MSE reduction, 97.5% interval [11.3398, 21.4430]%, positive in all five seeds. It does not establish incremental prediction of future/excess-future tracking under this analysis.
3. **Does propagated q predict them better?** Not beyond covariance plus sensitivity. Future-tracking MSE reduction is 0.0905% [−0.1273, 0.2321]%; excess-future reduction is 0.0202% [−0.0632, 0.0877]%. Both fail the interval criterion. q alone predicts action discrepancy, but that is insufficient and its increment beyond the comparators is inconclusive.
4. **Does CR-EC-QAD beat uniform?** Untested. The failed signal gate explicitly prohibits proceeding to this training experiment; an untrained method has no measured performance.
5. **Does CR-EC-QAD beat its exact shuffled control?** Untested for the same reason. No estimator-specific training benefit can be claimed.
6. **Are effects consistent across student seeds?** Future-tracking q increments are positive in 4/5 seeds, excess-future in 3/5. Intervals still cross zero. Closed-loop student performance varies; seed 19 has two SIGNAL failures. No seed is excluded or reselected.
7. **Does the result survive held-out fault severities/onsets?** SIGNAL scenarios use validation severity/onset ranges disjoint from TRAIN and separate IDs from checkpoint selection. The stricter final TEST scenarios remain unopened, so no final-test generalization claim is made.
8. **Is the mechanism estimator-specific?** Not established. Generic policy sensitivity explains substantial instantaneous discrepancy predictability; this analysis does not establish the extra value needed to justify aligned covariance-conditioned training. It also does not experimentally prove that generic reweighting causes the effects, since no Study 2 weighting intervention was run.
9. **What is supported?** Bounded Torch/NumPy action semantics; physical/reference-only deployment without PID; mathematically checked full-covariance propagation; disjoint whole-episode selection and signal analysis; two accepted teachers out of three; five independently optimized student seeds; and the target-specific quantitative findings above. Teacher seed 7 was fixed by a first-passing rule before student outcomes.
10. **What is not supported?** CR-EC-QAD superiority, estimator-specific reweighting benefits, causal compression-criticality, safety guarantees, real-flight robustness, hardware speed/energy benefits, final-TEST performance, or a universal claim that covariance can never be useful.

The appropriate endpoint is this negative/inconclusive mechanism result, not a revised loss. Study 1 conclusions remain unchanged. The negative teacher and student outcomes are retained.
