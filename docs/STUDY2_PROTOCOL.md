# Study 2 preregistered protocol

Study 1 is immutable at tag `study1-complete-20261109`. Study 2 uses `configs/study2.yaml`, `experiments/run_study2.py`, `learning/study2.py`, prefixed tables/documents and `results/study2/`. No Study 1 results or sources are replaced. This protocol is hashed before training or inspection of Study 2 outcomes.

## Fixed architecture and action semantics

The existing 16 physical/reference features, 16→32→32→4 architecture, aircraft, UKF, sensors, reference maneuvers and failure scoring remain fixed. The bounded action is `(tanh(latent)+[0,0,0,1])*[1,1,1,0.5]`. One function implements this definition for PyTorch and NumPy, including gradients, imitation, distillation, validation, deployment and Jacobians. Raw final affine outputs are latent values, never actuator labels. Teacher latent affine normalization initializes from inverse-tanh of expert actions clipped to ±0.999 only for initialization statistics; labels and losses use actual legal actuator commands. Final commands already satisfy bounds before the runner's redundant safety clamp. The common plant startup throttle limit remains an actuator characteristic, identical for all policies.

No P, NIS, q, Jacobian, PID hidden state or expert command enters deployed features. The unmodified runner does not instantiate PID for neural deployment. Study 2 checkpoint metadata requires the new action schema; Study 1 checkpoints are not converted.

## Scenarios, selection and teachers

TRAIN, VALIDATION and TEST use new disjoint scenario IDs (bases 110000, 120000–140000, 150000). VALIDATION has three disjoint subsets: SELECT (one episode per condition), TEACHER_GATE (two), SIGNAL (20). Config records seeds, fault intervals and counts. TRAIN has eight episodes per condition. Initial conditions, sensor noise, fault signs/axes, command amplitudes and timings vary as in Study 1. All episodes last up to 180 seconds. Four conditions: nominal, gyro bias, GPS dropout, pitot bias. TEST has 20 episodes per condition with stronger faults and later onsets than training/validation. No TEST outcome is read before decisions are frozen; test rollout is explicitly locked.

Three FP32 teachers use seeds 7,19,43, 180 epochs, candidates 60/120/180. Five four-bit uniform students use seeds 7,19,43,61,89, 100 epochs, candidates 25/50/75/100. Adam lr=.001, batch=1024. Fixed identical validation scenarios select the minimum mean closed-loop normalized tracking score, including the unchanged failure penalty; ties choose the earliest epoch. Offline action MSE is recorded, never the selection criterion. All candidates and optimizer/RNG resumes are preserved. Teacher gates separately require nominal and off-nominal score ≤1.5 times the matched expert mean and zero failures over the eight gate episodes. All three teachers are reported. The primary teacher is the first passing in the declared seed order, not the best downstream EC-QAD outcome. If none pass, stop and report this blocker rather than tuning on TEST.

Students initialize from that same frozen teacher and vary minibatch order by seed. Candidate training and selection do not load TEST. Demonstrations and normalization use TRAIN only; SELECT expert trajectories supply offline diagnostics. Student seeds are optimization replications, not independent aircraft or teacher replications.

## Full covariance propagation

The source of truth is the unchanged `policy_features(nav,ref)`. UKF state ordering is `[pn,pe,pd,vn,ve,vd,roll,pitch,yaw,bgx,bgy,bgz,bax,bay,baz]`. With reference, initial altitude origin, latest raw gyro and pitot reading held fixed, altitude=h0−pd and body rates=gyro−bg. Thus G includes altitude derivatives 1/30 and −1/300; wrapped heading-error derivative −2; roll/pitch scales 2/5; gyro-bias derivatives −1,−1,−2; sin/cos derivatives cos(yaw),−sin(yaw). All reference-feature rows and pitot-derived feature rows have zero derivative. Velocity and accelerometer-bias columns are zero because the current policy does not directly consume them. Full P correlations among the retained directions are propagated, not dropped.

This is conditional UKF uncertainty, not total sensor/actuator variance: independent current pitot/gyro measurement noise outside P is not invented. Angle-error derivatives are local except at the ±π branch discontinuity; tests avoid that nondifferentiable point and check wrapping on both sides. Logs use 25 Hz, an integer submultiple of the 50 Hz controller, so saved features and P share a control instant. This changes logging only, not plant/control timing.

`Sigma_z=G P G^T`; the bounded FP32 teacher's automatic derivative `J=d u/d z` yields `Sigma_u=J Sigma_z J^T`. The sole primary physical score is `q=trace(Sigma_u)`. Diagnostics record old C, trace(P), unscaled-feature ||J||_F, and four actuator variances. Symmetry, PSD roundoff, zero-covariance/insensitivity limits, analytic-versus-finite-difference G, and local empirical actuator covariance are tested. No determinants, maximum eigenvalues, q powers or score selection are allowed. Eigenvalues are validation diagnostics only.

## Signal analysis and gate before any new loss

Run the teacher and five uniform four-bit students on VALIDATION_SIGNAL. Use the same action-discrepancy norm, mean five-second future normalized squared tracking error, and paired teacher-relative excess future error as Study 1. Require a full five-second observed future; failed episodes supply only valid prefixes. At 1 Hz, compare old C, raw trace(P) diagnostically, ||J||_F, log1p(q), and NIS. The log1p(q) representation is fixed before observation; it does not define an alternative physical score. NIS is diagnostic only.

Use five whole-scenario folds (episode index modulo five); all seeds and adjacent timesteps of a scenario remain in its fold. Fit fixed ridge 1e-6 to asinh targets using fold-local feature normalization. Controls include category, active fault/severity and interactions, gyro axis, time/time², onset/duration, attitude magnitudes, current truth-based tracking severity, references and student-seed indicators. Fault metadata and truth are analysis-only covariates, never deployable uncertainty signals. Compute equal-episode MSE and hierarchical paired bootstrap over student seeds and shared scenario groups stratified by condition (2,000 draws, fixed seed). Intervals condition on fixed out-of-fold fits. Per-seed directions are reported.

The primary contrast is adding q beyond BOTH C and ||J||_F. The go gate requires positive mean incremental value, a strictly positive lower bound of the 97.5% two-sided interval, and at least four of five positive seed directions, for at least one of the TWO prespecified future/excess-future targets. The interval adjustment guards the two primary comparisons; action discrepancy alone cannot pass. Other comparisons are diagnostic. This restricted model can fail to detect nonlinear information; absence of significance is not proof of universal absence.

If the gate fails, STOP new weighting work. Produce the negative verdict and all executed validation metrics, keep TEST unopened, and label CR comparisons untested—not zero effects. This conditional stopping rule takes precedence over the requested downstream method grid.

If the gate passes, freeze a separate training-stage manifest before TEST: CR weighting uses precisely the Study 1 covariance-only transform on log1p(q), clipped standardized score ±3, multiplier .5, exponential with normalizing offset, [0.5,2] bounds and population mean one. Controls must include uniform, old covariance, exact covariance shuffle, CR and exact CR shuffle, plus existing fault-balance and sensitivity comparators. Every method uses the same candidate interval and closed-loop selection. Exact sorted weight equality is required. TEST is released once after these decisions. No fusion or TEST-driven tuning is allowed.

## Execution and limits

Run `.venv/bin/python experiments/run_study2.py freeze`, then stages `collect`, `teachers`, `teacher_gate`, `students`, `signals`, `analyze`; default six simulation workers, configurable by `--workers`. Stages resume completed work. Existing dependency lock/environment and aircraft provenance remain unchanged. Metrics include normalized score, physical RMSE and peak errors, failures, constraints, control effort and action discrepancy. Reported simulated variance is not a safety guarantee; fake quantization is not hardware speed/energy measurement. Generic reweighting remains an explanation unless the aligned CR method consistently beats its exact distribution-matched shuffle in independent student seeds.
